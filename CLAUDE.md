# Fate Grail War: notes for Claude

- The game ships as ONE self-contained HTML page (published as a claude.ai artifact). `node tools/build.js` joins `src/engine/*.js` then `src/ui/*.js` (name order) into one IIFE inside `src/page/template.html`, with `src/page/style.css` inlined. Write plain ES5-style browser JS: no modules, no imports, no npm dependencies.
- `src/engine/` must stay DOM-free: tests and tools load it with `new Function` (see `tests/engine.js`). Effects reach the UI only through the `FX` hook object, which the UI fills in.
- Fixed timestep `STEP = 1/60`; `simStep(dt)` advances the war. `G` is the live game state; `G.f` the fighters (servants and masters), `G.ai[i]` their AI or null for a human.
- Run `npm test` before every commit. Add a test in `tests/rules.test.js` for each new rule.
- Balance: use `npm run sim` (many AI wars) and change numbers from its report, not by feel alone. The owner cares most that the game is fun.
- Canon servants and masters are drawn as original top-down sprites. Masters and unhelmeted servants use `mh: true` heads (King Arthur's head is hand-drawn the same way) (`mhead`: a crown of hair with a fringe, the face only a crescent at the front) and draw long hair in `over` (on top of the shoulders), never as a `back` blob under the body. King Arthur wears a blue dress and cloak under silver plate (her blade is silver with a gold hilt); her wind effects stay pale; at the owner's request her Excalibur (NP) is gold, and her blade shows gold only while Strike Air or the NP reveal it.
- After changing the game, rebuild and commit `dist/fuyuki-war.html` too, so the repo always holds a playable build.
- Arena mode lives in the main game (`src/ui/25-arena.js`): duel/ffa/teams/master duels use `newGame()` with `SERV_DUEL` (duel-length stats); master-and-servant pairs use `newWar({ map, quick: true })`. `node tools/build.js` also writes `arena/servant-duel.html`: the same game opening on the Arena setup (`ARENA_ENTRY`), so Arena and the war always share one set of sprites in `src/ui/10-sprites.js`. Inline menu thumbnails come from `thumb()`, which gives each copy its own gradient ids (a copy inside a hidden panel would otherwise blank the gradients of every later copy).
- The war map is `MAPS.city` (`src/engine/12-city.js`, drawn by `src/ui/81-city-draw.js`): roads, zones and buildings as data; buildings collide as rectangles (`RECTS`, `rectsIn`, `segRect`), trees as circles. Interiors sit east of `cityW` and are reached through `MAP.doors`; AI routes on a road graph (`src/engine/13-citynav.js`, `navPath`). `assignHomes` puts each master at their lore home (Kiritsugu takes the Einzbern castle when no Einzbern does) and builds extra estates on `MAP.plots`. Every base has an interior and every master summons inside it: canon rooms have fixed doors; estates and backup safehouses get one of the spare rooms `MAP.estates` (est0-est3) through `giveEstate`, which adds a `gen` door on the plot (the nav graph is rebuilt when those change). By day, resting anywhere in one's own rooms heals (`inOwnHome`). The old compact map (`MAPS.fuyuki`) is used by Arena's Quick Grail War. Ryuudou Temple stands alone in the Mount Enzou woods at the north-west (forest out to the avenue at x 3000, nothing built beside it), with a lake behind it (a `pond` zone plus a hidden `water` block) and the Great Cavern's mouth on the hillside west of the stairs, reached by a forest path (keep it `reserve`d so trees stay off it).
- Blocks with `over: true` (gates, arches, the arcade roof, the bridge arch) are also `walk: true`: they never collide and are drawn after the fighters by `drawOverhead` (`src/ui/82-city-over.js`), fading while someone is under them. Doors, yards and summoning circles are drawn there too.
- A city war opens with the summoning (`src/ui/83-intro.js`): the sim waits while the player's master chants at `MAP.spots[home]` (`summonSpot`), then the servant appears. Speech bubbles come from `FX.say(f, key)` and the `LINES` table there; write original lines.
- Hit-stop is per fighter (`hitPause` sets `f.hs` on attacker and defender; `stepF` and their projectiles wait it out). The whole-screen freeze (`hitstopAt`, `G.hitstop`) is only for kills, God Hand, servant NP hits and parries a human is in, and only near the viewer. Shake, slow-motion, banners and sounds are local too: `FX.near` in the engine, `hearVol` ranges in the UI. The AI adds a cross-zone penalty (`zoneGap`) so it walks through doors instead of chasing enemies through walls.
- Shirou (`fightsServants`) skips the masters' half damage against servants, and his AI joins his servant's fight while healthy.
- Nights and days (`src/engine/58-clock.js`, drawn by `src/ui/84-night.js`): `G.clock` runs Night 1, Day 2, Night 2 ... up to the last night (`CLOCK.lastNight` long), whose midnight flows straight into the untimed Grail phase (`c.final`, banner "The Grail descends", HUD "NIGHT n · MIDNIGHT"; no dawn between). `opts.nights`; tests pass `nights: 1` for the old always-final behaviour. No civilians at night (`CIV.night` 0); exposure decays slowly and `duskSightings` marks a team the town saw misbehave as night falls. Night 1 is for scouting (`NIGHT1_EAR` shrinks how far fights are heard). Every phase draws an event while two teams stand (`EVT.dayChance` 1). Own home heals at any hour (`HOME_REGEN`). Before the final night AI fighters only target what they `knows` (close, partner-seen, hit them, rumored or sanctioned), hunt toward heard fights (`fightNoise`) and `huntPoint`s, and break off wounded; by day they rest at home (heals). Civilians (`G.civ`) are witnesses only: `witness` raises `G.expo[team]`; the Church reacts at `EXPO.rumor`, `EXPO.warn` and `EXPO.max` (sanction reveals the team). `npm run sim` reports night dead time.
- Engine and UI share one scope after the build: never give an engine function a name the UI already uses (the audio `noise()` was once shadowed, and the UI render loop `draw` once replaced the event deck draw, so no events ran in real wars). `tools/build.js` now refuses to build on a top-level name clash (`nameClashes`, also a test).
- Bounded fields, true names and day tasks live in `src/engine/59-day.js` (drawn by `src/ui/86-day.js`). `G.fields[team]` is each home's field (`lvl`/`max`; Mage family starts higher; a Caster servant makes it a `temple`, +1 level and +1 max). Enemies inside are felt (`G.seenBy` feeds `knowsExact`), slowed, and at level 3+ struck by bolts; a concealed Assassin (`f.cnc`, the Spirit button for Assassins) is never felt. `G.tn[team]` holds known true names: learned by seeing an NP or investigating a battle site; +10% damage against a known servant, and the HUD shows the class until then. By day `G.tasks` (fortify, ley lines, battle sites, school for students) complete by standing in the circle uncontested; `aiDayGoal` picks one for AI masters.
- Events live in `src/engine/61-events.js` (drawn by `src/ui/87-events.js`). `G.event` holds at most one at a time. Each war deals a night deck and a day deck (`NIGHT_DECK`, `DAY_DECK`, `buildDecks`, never repeats); a lore host alive moves an event up, otherwise a stand-in hosts it (`loreHost`). Nights: Challenge (host at the docks; unanswered = host regains a seal), Church Bounty, Horror in the River (boss in `G.mobs` with `boss: true`, truce near it, exposure for all at dawn if alive), False Death (`G.ghost`: the team is unknown until dawn), Berserker Unchained (`f.rampT`). Days (`EVT.dayChance`): Sealed School, King's Banquet (Iskandar, else the most kingly servant, else the Church meeting), Overseer's Summons. Final night: the Grail at one of `GRAIL_SITES`, pure (heals at its heart) or corrupted (mud pools, madness near the center). Truce zones: `truceZone`/`inTruce`/`truceStops`. Fighters' blows and shots also hit `G.mobs` (`hitMobsArc`, `hitMobsAt`; masters deal double to familiars).
- Second event batch in `src/engine/62-events2.js` (drawn by `src/ui/88-events2.js`): the Demolition (`EVT2.demo`; Kiritsugu, else the deadliest master, plants charges by hand at a rival home, Kayneth first; defenders disarm; a blown home becomes `rubble`, its doors are `ruined` and the team moves to a level-1 backup safehouse on a free plot), the Shadow (night 2+, after servant deaths; cannot be hurt, NPs push it back, swallows the weakened, forces a black Grail), the Festival (day; triple witnesses at the arcade, a one-time relic task), and the Great Cavern (`cavern` room under the temple, door at the cave mouth; a rare final site, always corrupted; anyone outside withers after a grace period).

- Pacts in `src/engine/65-pacts.js` (drawn by `src/ui/89d-pacts.js`): `G.pacts` joins two teams until dawn (`pactTeams` in `enemiesOf` and `hurt`; shared sight via `pactSees`; fields let partners through). While an offer or an AI's answer is pending the two teams parley (`pactParley`: AI fighters drop each other from `enemiesOf`); a human striking them withdraws it, and accepting only needs `pactCanAccept` (not `pactOpen`, so earlier blows never void a standing offer).
