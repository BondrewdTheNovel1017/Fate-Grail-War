# Fate Grail War: notes for Claude

- The game ships as ONE self-contained HTML page (published as a claude.ai artifact). `node tools/build.js` joins `src/engine/*.js` then `src/ui/*.js` (name order) into one IIFE inside `src/page/template.html`, with `src/page/style.css` inlined. Write plain ES5-style browser JS: no modules, no imports, no npm dependencies.
- `src/engine/` must stay DOM-free: tests and tools load it with `new Function` (see `tests/engine.js`). Effects reach the UI only through the `FX` hook object, which the UI fills in.
- Fixed timestep `STEP = 1/60`; `simStep(dt)` advances the war. `G` is the live game state; `G.f` the fighters (servants and masters), `G.ai[i]` their AI or null for a human.
- Run `npm test` before every commit. Add a test in `tests/rules.test.js` for each new rule.
- Balance: use `npm run sim` (many AI wars) and change numbers from its report, not by feel alone. The owner cares most that the game is fun.
- Canon servants and masters are drawn as original top-down sprites. Masters use `mh: true` heads (`mhead`: a crown of hair with a fringe, the face only a crescent at the front) and draw long hair in `over` (on top of the shoulders), never as a `back` blob under the body. King Arthur wears a blue dress and cloak under silver plate (her blade is silver with a gold hilt); her wind effects stay pale; at the owner's request her Excalibur (NP) is gold, and her blade shows gold only while Strike Air or the NP reveal it.
- After changing the game, rebuild and commit `dist/fuyuki-war.html` too, so the repo always holds a playable build.
- Arena mode lives in the main game (`src/ui/25-arena.js`): duel/ffa/teams/master duels use `newGame()` with `SERV_DUEL` (duel-length stats); master-and-servant pairs use `newWar({ map, quick: true })`. `node tools/build.js` also writes `arena/servant-duel.html`: the same game opening on the Arena setup (`ARENA_ENTRY`), so Arena and the war always share one set of sprites in `src/ui/10-sprites.js`. Inline menu thumbnails come from `thumb()`, which gives each copy its own gradient ids (a copy inside a hidden panel would otherwise blank the gradients of every later copy).
- The war map is `MAPS.city` (`src/engine/12-city.js`, drawn by `src/ui/81-city-draw.js`): roads, zones and buildings as data; buildings collide as rectangles (`RECTS`, `rectsIn`, `segRect`), trees as circles. Interiors sit east of `cityW` and are reached through `MAP.doors`; AI routes on a road graph (`src/engine/13-citynav.js`, `navPath`). `assignHomes` puts each master at their lore home (Kiritsugu takes the Einzbern castle when no Einzbern does) and builds extra estates on `MAP.plots`. Every base has an interior and every master summons inside it: canon rooms have fixed doors; estates and backup safehouses get one of the spare rooms `MAP.estates` (est0-est3) through `giveEstate`, which adds a `gen` door on the plot (the nav graph is rebuilt when those change). By day, resting anywhere in one's own rooms heals (`inOwnHome`). The old compact map (`MAPS.fuyuki`) is used by Arena's Quick Grail War.
- Blocks with `over: true` (gates, arches, the arcade roof, the bridge arch) are also `walk: true`: they never collide and are drawn after the fighters by `drawOverhead` (`src/ui/82-city-over.js`), fading while someone is under them. Doors, yards and summoning circles are drawn there too.
- A city war opens with the summoning (`src/ui/83-intro.js`): the sim waits while the player's master chants at `MAP.spots[home]` (`summonSpot`), then the servant appears. Speech bubbles come from `FX.say(f, key)` and the `LINES` table there; write original lines.
- Hit-stop is per fighter (`hitPause` sets `f.hs` on attacker and defender; `stepF` and their projectiles wait it out). The whole-screen freeze (`hitstopAt`, `G.hitstop`) is only for kills, God Hand, servant NP hits and parries a human is in, and only near the viewer. Shake, slow-motion, banners and sounds are local too: `FX.near` in the engine, `hearVol` ranges in the UI. The AI adds a cross-zone penalty (`zoneGap`) so it walks through doors instead of chasing enemies through walls.
- Shirou (`fightsServants`) skips the masters' half damage against servants, and his AI joins his servant's fight while healthy.
- Nights and days (`src/engine/58-clock.js`, drawn by `src/ui/84-night.js`): `G.clock` runs Night 1, Day 2, Night 2 ... and a final night with no time limit (`CLOCK`, `opts.nights`; tests pass `nights: 1` for the old always-final behaviour). Before the final night AI fighters only target what they `knows` (close, partner-seen, hit them, rumored or sanctioned), hunt toward heard fights (`fightNoise`) and `huntPoint`s, and break off wounded; by day they rest at home (heals). Civilians (`G.civ`) are witnesses only: `witness` raises `G.expo[team]`; the Church reacts at `EXPO.rumor`, `EXPO.warn` and `EXPO.max` (sanction reveals the team). `npm run sim` reports night dead time.
- Engine and UI share one scope after the build: never give an engine function a name the UI already uses (the audio `noise()` was once shadowed).
- Bounded fields, true names and day tasks live in `src/engine/59-day.js` (drawn by `src/ui/86-day.js`). `G.fields[team]` is each home's field (`lvl`/`max`; Mage family starts higher; a Caster servant makes it a `temple`, +1 level and +1 max). Enemies inside are felt (`G.seenBy` feeds `knowsExact`), slowed, and at level 3+ struck by bolts; a concealed Assassin (`f.cnc`, the Spirit button for Assassins) is never felt. `G.tn[team]` holds known true names: learned by seeing an NP or investigating a battle site; +10% damage against a known servant, and the HUD shows the class until then. By day `G.tasks` (fortify, ley lines, battle sites, school for students) complete by standing in the circle uncontested; `aiDayGoal` picks one for AI masters.
- Events live in `src/engine/61-events.js` (drawn by `src/ui/87-events.js`). `G.event` holds at most one at a time. Each war deals a night deck and a day deck (`NIGHT_DECK`, `DAY_DECK`, `buildDecks`, never repeats); a lore host alive moves an event up, otherwise a stand-in hosts it (`loreHost`). Nights: Challenge (host at the docks; unanswered = host regains a seal), Church Bounty, Horror in the River (boss in `G.mobs` with `boss: true`, truce near it, exposure for all at dawn if alive), False Death (`G.ghost`: the team is unknown until dawn), Berserker Unchained (`f.rampT`). Days (`EVT.dayChance`): Sealed School, King's Banquet (Iskandar, else the most kingly servant, else the Church meeting), Overseer's Summons. Final night: the Grail at one of `GRAIL_SITES`, pure (heals at its heart) or corrupted (mud pools, madness near the center). Truce zones: `truceZone`/`inTruce`/`truceStops`. Fighters' blows and shots also hit `G.mobs` (`hitMobsArc`, `hitMobsAt`; masters deal double to familiars).
- Second event batch in `src/engine/62-events2.js` (drawn by `src/ui/88-events2.js`): the Demolition (`EVT2.demo`; Kiritsugu, else the deadliest master, plants charges by hand at a rival home, Kayneth first; defenders disarm; a blown home becomes `rubble`, its doors are `ruined` and the team moves to a level-1 backup safehouse on a free plot), the Shadow (night 2+, after servant deaths; cannot be hurt, NPs push it back, swallows the weakened, forces a black Grail), the Festival (day; triple witnesses at the arcade, a one-time relic task), and the Great Cavern (`cavern` room under the temple, door at the cave mouth; a rare final site, always corrupted; anyone outside withers after a grace period).
- Animation layer in `src/ui/89-anim.js`: `fighterXform` (walk sway, lean, wind-up, lunge, dodge stretch, recoil, stagger, death fall/dissolve) wraps each `drawFighter`; `drawGroundFx` (NP circles, Sakura's shadow), `drawFighterFx` (Illya's birds, Arthur's wind wrap), `drawProjX` (wind, bird, worm, shadow, gem, key, needle shapes; tracer muzzle flash).
- Rule Breaker and God Hand in `src/engine/63-rulebreak.js`: Medea's NP landing on an enemy servant in war mode calls `ruleBreak` (team switched for `RB.dur`, `trueTeam` kept for win checks, a human's servant gets a temporary AI, no NP while turned, `aiTurned` goes for its own master); `momLock` stops her momentum building after the NP. `godHandTick` restores God Hand after `GH_REGEN` seconds without dealing or taking hits. Dialogue in `src/ui/89b-dialogue.js`: `MORE_LINES` merged into `LINES`, rival `PAIRS` on first engagement, `BANQ_SCRIPTS` at the King's table.
- Bonds in `src/engine/64-family.js` and `src/ui/89c-bonds.js`: `FAMILY` pairs (Kiritsugu, Irisviel, Illya, Shirou, Rin, Sakura, Kariya) never kill each other with the first lethal blow (`familyHold` leaves 1 HP, once per attacker and target); an AI attacker then often spares them (`G.spare`, a targeting penalty). Kiritsugu spares nobody but Illya, Irisviel and Shirou: keep his FAMILY pairs to those three. UI tables: `MORE_PAIRS` (enemy exchanges), `TRUCE_PAIRS` (side by side in a truce), `HESIT` (the stopped hand), `KILLQ` (last words; `FX.say(killer, 'kill', victim)`).
- Pacts in `src/engine/65-pacts.js` (drawn by `src/ui/89d-pacts.js`): `G.pacts` joins two teams until dawn (`pactTeams` in `enemiesOf` and `hurt`; shared sight via `pactSees`; fields let partners through). One per team, none on the final night or under `PACT.minTeams`, never the two strongest, cooldown after; `outnumbered` softens blows on a lone team; AI pacts never close in on the player (`pactAim`). Will is per character (`PACT_M`, `PACT_S`, `pactWill`): Kiritsugu only with Illya, Irisviel or Shirou; Gilgamesh only with Kirei's team, and `gilBetrayTick` (once a war, in a pact or truce, Kirei without a servant) has him kill his master and contract Kirei. `TREACH` decides who breaks a pact; breaking (`breakPact`) adds exposure and a reveal. The player uses the Pact button (P): ask, accept, or press twice to break. While an offer or an AI's answer is pending the two teams parley (`pactParley`: AI fighters drop each other from `enemiesOf`); a human striking them withdraws it, and accepting only needs `pactCanAccept` (not `pactOpen`, so earlier blows never void a standing offer).
- A human in the war only perceives a target through `aimFoe` (`src/engine/50-fighters.js`): known (`knowsExact`), same zone, within `AIM_R`. Auto-facing, the HUD target card, the camera lean, the off-screen arrow and Tab cycling all go through it, so nothing points at enemies across the city. AI fighters still use `f.foe` directly.
- Menus: `src/ui/95-shell.js` runs the menu shell (title screen, main menu, Codex, Settings, and the stepped War and Arena setup pages, `SHELL.page`), its sounds (`SND.ui*`), keyboard navigation, the animated night canvas `#menuBg` (paused in game and with Menu motion off, `body.calm`) and `beginFlourish` before a battle starts. `renderSetup` (20-menu) and `renderArena` still fill the pages; `setFeat` draws the featured figure on the character-select pages. The Codex Characters tab comes from `LORE` and `MLORE` in `src/ui/96-lore.js` (original short histories and wishes; keep the wording our own). Markup in `src/page/template.html`, styles at the end of `src/page/style.css`.
- Rho Aias and Enkidu in `src/engine/66-rho.js` (drawn by `src/ui/89e-rho.js`): EMIYA (7 petals, `RHO`) and Shirou once his Trace reaches `TRACE.rho` (4 petals) block whole frontal projectiles while guarding, NPs included, via `rhoBlock` in `hurt`; `rhoCost` sets petals per shot; `rhoTick` regrows them; Rule Breaker (`a.strip`) dissolves Rho Aias and the Hot Gates with `FX.shieldBroken`. Shirou's Trace (`TRACE`) grows all war (base rate plus fighting), Unlimited Blade Works held until Night 2. Gilgamesh's skill is Enkidu (`bind`, `enkiduBind`; `DIVINE` servants are held 1.5x longer).
- Online play: `src/ui/90-net.js` (the room, the lobby, starting matches, inputs), `91-netsync.js` (replication) and `92-lobby.js` (the lobby screen). Host-authoritative over claude.ai `room` presence (4 KiB per room, about 40 sends a second per page): the lobby room carries seats and guests' inputs; the host's state goes out in rooms `-f` (fast fighter fields, shots, zones), `-m` (all other fighter fields, rotating), `-s0..2` (generic `G` keys packed by size), `-c` (civilians, familiars) and `-e` (FX calls with sequence numbers, replayed on guests). `nenc`/`ndec` turn fighters, familiars and attack tables (`nsRegistry`) into numbers. Guests rebuild the same war (`netBuild`, same teams, spawns and `plots`) and predict their own movement (`nsPredict`). Seats: every servant and master; empty seats are AI. Test with `#localnet` (a BroadcastChannel stand-in room) in several tabs served over http. When adding state to `G`, check it fits in the `-s` rooms (`NET.dropped`).
