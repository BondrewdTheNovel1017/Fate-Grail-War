# Approved ideas (Nov 26)

Mirrors the Claude Doc "Fate Grail War Roadmap". The aim: a war of knowing; each idea feeds one loop, hide, scout, find, strike.

- **The war's chronicle (Phase 1, cheap):** the end screen tells the war as a timeline (first blood, pacts and betrayals, seal rescues, NPs revealed, kills, who took the Grail), built from the `FX` events the engine already sends.
- **True names matter more (Phase 1):** besides +10% damage, a known servant's NP wind-up shows a moment earlier, and the War Board gives a one-line note on how to face it. Battle sites become worth reading.
- **Rivalries as flavor (with dialogue work):** canon rivals (Kayneth/Waver, Kariya/Tokiomi, Kiritsugu/Kirei...) get lines on meeting, on the kill and in the chronicle. No stat rewards.
- **Hidden homes (Phase 2a):** rivals cannot see bounded fields; you find one by walking in (the owner hears the alarm), by a familiar seeing the shimmer, or by rumor. Estates and safehouses look like ordinary houses, with more lookalike plots than teams. Mage families have a known address but start stronger (higher field, a free upgrade pick); others start hidden and weaker; Veil hides whether a known home is occupied. Kiritsugu can keep the castle as a decoy and work from a hidden house. Homes are found by seeing doors used, following a wounded enemy, familiars, battle-site clues, the Overseer's Summons, and dawn rumors from high exposure. The War Board shows unknown / district rumored / known. The AI finds homes the same way; `npm run sim` checks pacing.
- **Familiars you steer (Phase 2a):** mage families (Rin, Tokiomi, Sakura, Kariya, Kayneth, Irisviel, Illya) plus Waver, Kiritsugu, Kirei; not Shirou, Ryuunosuke, Kuzuki, Caren. Only from home; the master sits in a trance; costs mana, about a minute. Sees and marks; cannot attack; can be shot down (reveals the sender); sets off fields. Looks: Tohsaka jewel birds, Einzbern wire birds, Matou insects, Kayneth's mercury (slow, tough), Kiritsugu's camera bat (cannot see spirit form, unfelt by fields). The servant follows its order meanwhile.
- **Traps for certain masters (Phase 2a):** Kiritsugu's mines, ward traps for Kayneth and the mage families, Kirei's Black Key snares. Set by day at doors and chokepoints; faint glints to your own team; familiars spot them; walk over slowly to disarm; never kill outright; never on a summoning circle.
- **A clearer day and night rhythm (Phase 2a):** day prepares, night hunts; a dusk summary of what you learned since dawn.
- **Guided first night (Phase 3):** a scripted Night 1 teaching scouting, the War Board, pacts and seals.
- **Later, progression:** sidegrade unlocks between wars (familiars, traps, upgrades, alternate skills), the Codex filled by discovered true names, kept chronicles; an everything-unlocked option online.
