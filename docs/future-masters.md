# Future masters (designed, not built yet)

Rules for every new master: no canon-pairing bonuses (any master can be paired with any servant). Each gets one edge, one real cost, one skill and one signature, with health 85–125 and a mana pool in the existing range. Balance with the war and duel sims: each master's win rate within about ±5 points of the roster average; adjust health or one number on the edge, never add a bonus. Suggested build order: Caren, Bazett, Ryuunosuke, Shinji.

## Ryuunosuke Uryuu — Outsider (ADDED Nov 26)
- Health 95. No magecraft: no healing, shields or anything defensive.
- Edge: bounded fields cannot feel him. He earns mana by doing harm: a little per point of damage he or his servant deals, a lump per kill. The amounts are tuned in the sim so his average income across a war matches the median master's, and no more.
- Cost: no natural regeneration, pool capped at 100. Civilian kills bring the Church as for anyone. Only the first few civilian kills each day pay in full.
- Skill, Fade into the Crowd: by day, near civilians, he cannot be targeted from range. Nothing at night.
- Signature, Masterpiece: a grab from behind that executes a master below 40% health. Against servants it is only a weak cut.
- Summoning: his own storehouse scene (cosmetic only). He reads aloud from an old book, unsure the ritual is right; the circle he drew in red flares; eerie, not graphic.

## Shinji Matou — False Master
- Health 90. Weak fighter: book swings and a shadow-page lash. Small mana pool.
- Edge, Blood Fort (signature): a draining field centred on his servant that moves with it. It drains enemies fighting near the servant and feeds the servant's NP charge. The field holds only while Shinji stays within about 900 units of his servant, and collapses the moment Shinji takes damage (short cooldown to raise it again). Tuned so a servant inside it wins a trade it would otherwise draw, not more; never stronger than a Caster's field.
- Cost: his command seals are pages of the Book of False Attendant; dropping under 30% health burns one.
- Skill, Rider, Come: a short-cooldown recall of his servant.
- AI: the coward. Hangs at the edge of the leash, runs when approached, recalls when hunted. The intended loop: players stop fighting his servant and hunt him.

## Bazett Fraga McRemitz — Enforcer
- Health 115, strong guard. Full damage to servants, like Shirou and Kuzuki.
- Skill, Ansuz: a short rune blast in front of her that staggers. No healing.
- Signature, Fragarach (Answerer): a counter stance with a one-second window. The first damaging attack aimed at her in the window (light, heavy, projectile, damaging skill or Noble Phantasm) is cancelled; she dashes up to 450 units to that attacker and strikes only them. Against an NP, the caster loses their momentum as if interrupted; her counter damage is the same either way. Long cooldown (about 35 s, set by the sim). A wasted stance leaves her briefly open. Counterplay: bait it with a cheap jab or shot, then commit once it is on cooldown.

## Caren Hortensia — Church (ADDED Nov 26)
- Health 90, lowest damage of any master.
- Edge, Stigmata: within 700 units she senses concealed Assassins, rampaging Berserkers, the Shadow and corrupted things through walls, and her team sees them. Cost: a small health drain while something is in range, never below 40%.
- Skill, Shroud of Magdalene: a bind, 1.6 s on men and 0.8 s on women (needs a male/female flag on every character), tuned so the average hold matches Irisviel's Wire Bind.
- Signature, Sacrament: heals her and her servant, comparable to Rin's and Irisviel's heals.
- Story only, not stats: shares the Church with Kirei as family; Gilgamesh is willing to pact with her team; she can host the Overseer's Summons event. No reduced Church heat.
