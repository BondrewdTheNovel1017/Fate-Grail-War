# Bounded fields, safe zones, interiors (planned Nov 26)

Mirrors the Claude Doc "Fate Grail War Roadmap".

- **Fields shaped to the property (Phase 2, map):** each field is a shape in the map data (the lot's outline plus its rooms), not a circle: the whole Emiya yard, the Tohsaka grounds to the gate, the Einzbern forest edge, the temple precinct. The owner sees a faint edge along walls and fences.
- **Ward points that make sense:** stones picked up at the summoning circle and set at gate posts, wall corners, doors, the edge of the woods. Family stones: Tohsaka jewels; Einzbern silver wire knots and crystal; Matou worm-carved stones; Kayneth mercury vials; Emiya household Kiritsugu's bell wards (canon: the house's field only warns, so Shirou's starts as an alarm); Caster's temple rune-cut stones; Church Black Key stakes; others plain rune stones.
- **Safe zones shaped the same way:** the Church's sanctuary is its whole fenced yard and building; outside the gate is fair game. Same for every truce zone.
- **Fields that matter (Phase 1):** level 1 warns the owner and slows intruders; level 2 also stops intruders' mana regen and spirit-form healing; level 3 adds bolts every few seconds (a few percent of health); level 4+ stronger, more frequent bolts and no entering spirit form. Servants resist by class (Saber, Lancer, Archer high; Rider, Berserker some; Caster, Assassin little); masters take full. Base upgrades build on this. Check with `npm run sim`.
- **Outsiders (Phase 1):** no field, but heal and refill mana at home. Ryuunosuke keeps harm mana, no home refill. A Caster servant gives any team the temple field.
- **Accurate interiors (Phase 2, map):** Einzbern castle with more detail (great hall, chandeliers, long carpet, the stair to the courtyard, Irisviel's rooms, tall windows); Tohsaka manor with the basement workshop down a stair; Matou house with the worm pit below (bites anyone not a Matou); Emiya household with the dojo and the shed where Shirou summons; Ryuudou Temple's hall and quarters (Kuzuki's lore home in the Fifth War); the Church's chapel and rooms; Kayneth's hotel floor, then the abandoned factory after the Demolition; Waver's Mackenzie house.
