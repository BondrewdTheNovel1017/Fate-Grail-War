How the sandbox works
MastersEach team is a servant and a fragile master. If a master dies, their servant fades after 20 seconds. Archers last 120 seconds without one.
Spirit formServants dematerialize in 1.5 seconds (1 to return). Unseen, untouchable and faster, but cannot attack. A hit while changing form cancels it.
OrdersPlaying as the master, you order your servant: Protect, Charge, Hold, Don't attack, Retreat or Spirit form.
Command SealsThree seals, each used once: NP charges your servant's Noble Phantasm at once, Heal restores half its health, Recall brings it to your side from anywhere.
Mana linkEvery master has a mana pool that refills over time. A Noble Phantasm costs the master 40 mana (55 for a reality marble), and the master's own skill and signature spell cost some too. A materialized servant slowly drains its master, faster when they are far apart; spirit form costs nothing. A servant whose master runs dry deals less damage. Kariya pays any shortfall with his own blood, and the NP Command Seal pays the full cost.
Form ContractA servant who loses their master and a master who loses their servant can bind themselves anew: walk up to them and press Contract (F). Until then they won't fight each other. The new pair fights for the master's team and keeps the master's remaining Command Seals. A masterless servant can still release its Noble Phantasm, but it burns 6 seconds of the time it has left.
Master magecraftEvery master has a skill and a signature spell on the NP button with its own cooldown. Masters are fragile and deal half damage to servants. Kiritsugu's Origin Bullet burns a master's circuits: no skill or signature for 10 seconds.
Reality marblesUnlimited Blade Works and Ionioi Hetairoi pull everyone within range into a sealed world for 25 seconds, keeping where they stood. Outsiders can't see in or reach them. Spirit form escapes the pull, a Command Seal recall pulls a servant out, and Enuma Elish shatters it.
Shirou's TraceFighting fills his gauge. At 40 he learns Trace On: homing blades and Kanshou and Bakuya. At 100 he can open Unlimited Blade Works once, with homing swords that last longer than EMIYA's.
Long fightsServants are much tougher here and Noble Phantasms charge slowly, but hit harder when they land.
Target lockTap or click an enemy to lock on, or press Target to cycle. Tap empty ground to clear.
Fuyuki CityThe Mion River splits Miyama from Shinto, crossed by the red arch of Fuyuki Bridge, the Mion Bridge to the south and the railway bridge. Miyama has Ryuudou Temple on Mount Enzou, the Tohsaka and Matou houses on the hill, the church, Homurahara Academy, the Emiya residence and the shopping street; Shinto has the Hyatt, the Center Building, Verde, the station, Central Park, the harbor and the Einzbern forest. Buildings block movement, sight and shots.
Homes and interiorsEvery master starts at their lore home: Rin at the Tohsaka mansion, Sakura and Kariya at the Matou house, Illya and Irisviel at Einzbern Castle, Kirei at the church, Kayneth at the Hyatt, Waver at the Mackenzie house, Shirou at the Emiya residence, Kiritsugu at a safehouse. A second member of the same family gets a new estate somewhere in the city. Walk into the doors of Einzbern Castle, the Tohsaka mansion, the Matou house, the church or the Emiya residence to fight inside.

How the fight works
LightFast chain. Hitting a charging heavy knocks it out. Blocked by Block.
HeavyHold to charge, release to swing. Breaks a block. A well-timed dodge beats it and leaves the attacker open.
Block and parryHold to block, which drains stamina. Tap right before a hit lands to parry and stagger the attacker.
Purple warningsCannot be blocked or parried. Dodge them.
Noble PhantasmMomentum fills as you fight. At full, the NP is ready. If you are hit while it winds up, it is lost. Masters use a signature spell on a cooldown instead.
Arena paceArena fights use duel-length health and normal Noble Phantasm charging, so a match takes a few minutes. In Master and servant matches the war rules apply: mana, Command Seals and a fading servant if its master falls.
